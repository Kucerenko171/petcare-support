Support

How can we help?

🐶
Adding a pet
Tap the + button on the Pets tab. Fill in name, species, breed, and date of birth. You can add a photo from your library or take a new one.
📅
Events & reminders
Open a pet's profile and tap Add Event. Choose a category (vet visit, vaccine, medication…) and set a date. You'll receive a push notification as a reminder.
🤖
AI Symptom Checker
Describe your pet's symptoms in plain language. The AI provides an initial assessment — always follow up with a licensed veterinarian for diagnosis.
👑
Premium subscription
PetCare Premium unlocks unlimited pets, AI tools, PDF export, and vet reports. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in iOS Settings → Subscriptions.
🔔
Notifications not arriving
Go to iOS Settings → PetCare → Notifications and verify that Allow Notifications is enabled. Make sure Background App Refresh is also on.
🗑️
Deleting your account
Open the app → Profile → Delete Account. All your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Yan Silitskiy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the PetCare mobile application ("the App"). By using PetCare you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register or sign in with Google.
  • Pet profiles — names, species, breeds, dates of birth, and any photos you upload for your pets.
  • Health & event data — veterinary visits, vaccinations, medications, weights, and other events you record.
  • AI Symptom Checker inputs — symptom descriptions you type when using the AI analysis feature.
  • Push notification token — a device token used solely to deliver reminders and alerts you request.
  • Subscription status — whether your account has an active PetCare Premium subscription.
  • Usage data — basic app usage information (feature accessed, errors encountered) to improve the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone input, or any financial payment details.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and sync your pet data across devices.
  • To send push notification reminders for events you have scheduled.
  • To provide AI-powered symptom analysis via our backend AI service.
  • To process and validate your in-app subscription through RevenueCat.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app reliability.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-party services

PetCare uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your anonymous app user ID and subscription status. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Expo (by Expo Technology, Inc.) — powers push notifications. Expo receives your device push token to route notifications. expo.dev/privacy
  • Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In — if you choose social login, the respective platform provides us only with your name and email address.

Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use HTTPS for all data transmission and follow industry-standard practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app's Profile screen. Upon deletion, all personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Children's privacy

PetCare is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at yanuck00723@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date below and, where appropriate, notify you through the app.
